### fig1_core_validity_informativeness

E1, primary 95% level. Coverage and detection error bars are pointwise Wilson intervals over complete independent logs, not simultaneous bounds. Width medians/IQR are descriptive coordinate quantiles; nontriviality is distinct from a useful certified decision. The final panel uses maxima within each complete trial, and retains uninformative cells. COMET and the fully matched generic method are algebraically identical; their separate rows remain in the tables.

### fig2_query_locality

E2 mechanism, predeclared constant policy 0. Audited-first-half and global queries are shown separately, across every outside hub/overlap condition. Three pure residual-truncation layers isolate the costs: global envelope/global graph, local moments/global graph, and local moments/induced-support graph. COMET / matched generic additionally dispatches to sharp bounded certificates; its improvement over pure local truncation at the same coloring is a bounded-branch benefit, not a graph-localization benefit. The vertical coordinate is a design-determined normalized radius, not Monte Carlo uncertainty. No average over outside/global auditors is substituted for locality. All methods, four policies and auditors remain in empirical_coordinates.csv.

### fig3_paired_contrasts

E3, primary 95% level, constant changed-policy anchor and all-row auditor. Direct signed contrasts and subtraction of simultaneously certified, deduplicated unique levels share the same total error budget and logged data. Shown widths are descriptive per-query medians; reducing a policy change also changes its signal and feasible range, and does not by itself establish improved sign-certification power.

### fig4_gap_power_boundary

E4 whole-trial probabilities of certifying the primary query safe or violating its threshold. Shaded gamma<=0 points are null/safe settings, not positive-gap power. Error bars are pointwise Wilson 95% intervals. The 2r line is a design-only sufficient boundary on the coverage event; an off-axis boundary is reported numerically, not clipped into an apparent successful regime. All false-safe/false-violation counts and intervals remain in empirical_events.csv.

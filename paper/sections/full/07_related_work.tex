\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Off-policy and orthogonal estimation.}
Inverse-propensity and doubly robust estimators are standard tools for contextual bandits and sequential decision problems \citep{dudik2011doubly,jiang2016doubly,wang2017optimal,farajtabar2018more}. Cross-fitting and Neyman orthogonality allow flexible nuisance learners while keeping first-order target inference insensitive to small nuisance errors \citep{chernozhukov2018double,foster2019orthogonal}. \COMET\ adopts this orthogonal-score principle but changes the inferential object from one average policy value to a simultaneous family of policy--auditor moments, and changes the sampling model from independent bounded scores to weak-overlap, heavy-tailed, graph--time dependent logs.

\paragraph{Weak overlap and heavy tails.}
Strong overlap bounds the importance ratio uniformly, while weak-overlap work studies slower rates, truncation, or partial identification when this condition fails \citep{mehrabi2024weak,khan2024beyond}. Robust mean estimation provides exponential-confidence procedures under finite moments through median-of-means, truncation, and related estimators \citep{lugosi2019mean}. The contribution here is not the use of MOM in isolation. It is the combination of orthogonal counterfactual scores, an observable dependence design effect, simultaneous auditing, nuisance remainders, and downstream certified policy selection. Our experiments separately vary tail behavior from the outcome and from the importance ratio because the same empirical score tail can have different operational remedies.

\paragraph{Interference and dependent data.}
Exposure mappings and design-based estimators formalize causal effects when one unit's outcome depends on others' assignments \citep{aronow2017estimating,savje2021average}. Dependency-graph concentration controls sums of partly dependent variables by graph coloring or related fractional parameters \citep{janson2004large}; blocking and coupling are classical tools for mixing sequences \citep{merlevede2009bernstein}. \COMET\ uses these ideas as a logged-system interface: conflict edges are generated by slates, repeated entities, and declared interference neighborhoods, and the resulting design penalty is reported with every certificate. This differs from assuming an abstract effective sample size without showing how it is computed.

\paragraph{Auditing, calibration, and performative systems.}
Multicalibration asks predictions to be calibrated over many subpopulations \citep{hebertjohnson2018multicalibration}, while outcome indistinguishability gives a broader test-based view of prediction quality \citep{dwork2021outcome}. Performative prediction studies how deployment changes the data distribution \citep{perdomo2020performative}. COPF applies counterfactual exposure effects, residual OI, and online control to evolving-graph link recommendation \citep{li2026copf}. \COMET\ retains the useful residual-moment abstraction but removes the task-specific dependence on link prediction, fixed fairness slices, strict overlap, and one controller. Learned auditors are separated from certification, and policy repair is formulated directly through simultaneous uncertainty bounds.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
Open Bandit Dataset enables reproducible OPE comparisons using real logged propensities and multiple behavior policies \citep{saito2021open}. KuaiRand adds randomized exposures and rich sequential recommendation feedback \citep{gao2022kuairand}. TGB supplies realistic temporal graph structure and standardized streams \citep{huang2023tgb}. We use them for distinct validation roles, supplemented by controlled generators in which the full counterfactual truth and dependence graph are known.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper reframes robust counterfactual fairness on evolving graphs as a more general problem: estimating, testing, and acting on counterfactual moments from weak-overlap, heavy-tailed, dependent logs. \COMET\ supplies one orthogonal-score interface, an observable graph--time correction, robust simultaneous certificates, a sample-split mechanism for discovering hidden failure subgroups, and a pessimistic policy-repair rule. The framework makes uncertainty operational: inadequate overlap, dependence, nuisance error, and drift widen different parts of the certificate or force abstention. The remaining work is concrete and falsifiable---complete the sharp dependence proof and adaptive tail tuning, run coverage-centered experiments, and determine whether the predicted effective-sample-size scaling holds on realistic graph and recommendation logs.

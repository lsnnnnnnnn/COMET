# ICLR 2027 submission checklist

Current official milestones (recheck on the conference site before submission):

- Abstract deadline: September 18, 2026, 11:59 PM Anywhere on Earth.
- Full paper and supplementary-material deadline: September 25, 2026, Anywhere on Earth.
- Main text at submission: at most 9 pages.
- References do not count toward the limit; appendices may follow the bibliography.
- Submission is double blind; the main paper and supplementary material must not reveal author identity.
- AI use statement is required and does not count toward the page limit.
- Ethics and reproducibility statements are recommended and do not count toward the main-text limit under the current author guide.

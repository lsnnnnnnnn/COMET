\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The proved result is simultaneous counterfactual moment inference under an exogenous design, independent evaluation blocks, sufficient within-block dependency graphs, exact exposure propensities and fixed or independent-pilot nuisances. Finite-$p$ MOM and bounded-outcome graph variance supply different certificates with explicit constants and applicability conditions. A valid but full-range interval supports abstention, not a useful safety decision.

The analysis does not establish arbitrary adaptive pathwise coverage, mutually trained cross-fitting, unknown tail-scale fitting, hidden cross-block shocks, optimality for every sparse graph or long-run deployment safety. Exposure mappings and joint-policy compatibility require substantive modeling. Heavy-tail F may remain wholly vacuous. The manuscript's empirical claims must follow completed frozen results, and its broader discovery, repair and network program requires separate proofs and experiments.

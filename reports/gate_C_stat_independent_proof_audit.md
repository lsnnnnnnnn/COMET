## Finite-p MOM and exact-majority certificate

The centered score envelope applies conditional on D. Inside each independent color class, von Bahr–Esseen with constant at most 2 gives a p-moment bound. Convexity over chi possibly dependent color sums and division by n0 yield

\[
E|\overline Z_b-M_D|^p\le 2\nu_p^p(\chi/n_0)^{p-1}.
\]

Block means are centered at the same target because every block repeats the design and response law. A second application to L_g independent block means gives

\[
E|T_g-M_D|^p\le4\nu_p^p\{\chi/(L_g n_0)\}^{p-1}.
\]

Thus the registered radius `32^(1/p)*nu_p*(chi/(L_min*n0))^((p-1)/p)` controls each group failure at eta=1/8. This verifies `theory/exact_binomial_mom.md:18–59,63–83` and `comet/robust_mean.py:123–147`; the implementation uses every complete block and has L_min>=2 in every registered cell/level.

For independent heterogeneous group failures with probabilities at most eta, coordinatewise coupling to independent Bernoulli(eta) variables proves binomial domination. For odd K the median can fail only with at least `(K+1)/2` bad groups. Hence the exact selector's condition

\[
m\sum_{j=(K+1)/2}^K {K\choose j}(1/8)^j(7/8)^{K-j}\le\alpha
\]

is sufficient for simultaneous family coverage. This is the bound in `theory/exact_binomial_mom.md:89–118`, implemented with exact rational comparisons at `comet/robust_mean.py:58–120`. The old rule also remains valid: its even-K median failure is contained in at least K/2 bad groups, Hoeffding gives `exp(-9K/32)<=exp(-K/8)`, and the conservative `ceil(8 log(2m/alpha))` spends no more than alpha. Different methods and levels each have their own within-family guarantee; no claim of joint 95% coverage across all methods is made.

The heavy envelope is independently justified by

\[
k_p=E|\epsilon|^p=\frac{a\sigma^p}{a-p},\quad
E|Y-\widehat\mu|^p\le 2^{p-1}(1+k_p),\quad
\nu_p^p=2^{2p-1}\{1+2^{p-1}(1+k_p)\sum_s q_s^pe_s^{1-p}\}.
\]

The last step uses the valid general centering bound `E|Z-EZ|^p<=2^p E|Z|^p`, not the invalid raw-to-centered inequality from the original lower-bound proof. F has p=1.5<a=1.75, so k_p is finite. The formula in `comet/gate_c_contract.py:93–124` matches `theory/gate_c_heavy_tail_contract.md:118–162`; p>=a is refused. The bounded A–E formula removes the unnecessary heavy residual multiplier and is also valid. These envelopes use known class bounds, p, q/e and analytic scale, never a true response location, observed tail estimate or empirical variance.

## Numerical scope and remaining unproved claims

The numerical convention is already frozen in `theory/gate_c_heavy_tail_contract.md:216–237`. The positive Pareto grid is moment-dominated by the ideal continuous integral because its p-moment is a right Riemann sum of a decreasing function. The finite grid does not literally have infinite variance, and adding a floating response location is not an interval-arithmetic proof of exact last-bit centering. These are existing disclosed numerical limitations, not newly discovered changes to the registered model. No final-bit exact-confidence or true-random-generator claim is made here.

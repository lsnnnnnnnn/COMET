# D4 — non-replicated dependence and joint exposure (UNRUN; PROOF GATE)

Fresh domain: `COMET-D4-v1`. No certification or outcome generation until a new
proof-to-code contract is reviewed. Specify an actual joint logging law P(A|D)
and target law Q(A|D), supported on legal slates/network assignments. Compute
e_u(s)=P(g_u(A)=s|D) and q_u(s)=Q(g_u(A)=s|D) exactly by finite enumeration or a
proved dynamic program, preserving full joint-slate denominators. A two-item,
one-slot fixture must reject q_1(shown)=q_2(shown)=1; separate local marginals
do not by themselves define an implementable joint policy. If no Q exists,
label the target exposure-marginal functional and exclude policy-repair claims.

Construct each block from independent primitive random variables: slate-choice
seeds, local node/edge shocks and outcome innovations. For each score list its
entire primitive support, including assignment and any shared fitted variable.
Connect rows sharing any primitive. Conditional on legitimate exogenous D,
disjoint vertex sets with no edge then use disjoint independent collections;
this proves strong vector independence, not just zero covariance. Primitive
collections must also be disjoint across macro-blocks. Shared global shocks
must be modeled or refused. Original network adjacency is not automatically
this graph. Independent nuisance pilots are conditioned on before this proof.

Predeclare small exact-enumeration fixtures, coloring validation, incomplete
graph negative controls and oracle-exclusion tests. Then compare cliques to
non-copy overlapping-primitive designs at fixed N, m and overlap. Record actual
color upper bound, variance inflation and whether resulting intervals decide.
Graph mean supports heterogeneous block means; MOM requires stable block
means unless a separately proved correction is preregistered. A new theorem
is not inferred from matching empirical coverage.

No numerical replication budget is released until joint-policy and graph proof
gates pass. A prospective 1,000-log confirmatory grid requires fresh frozen
seeds and a separate engineering pilot. D4 is not an adaptive-history study.

\subsection{Exact majority calibration for independent-block MOM}
\label{sec:c0-exact-majority}

Let $\mathcal D$ be an exogenous design fixed before evaluation randomization,
possibly augmented by a nuisance pilot independent of the evaluation sample
conditional on that design. Conditional on $\mathcal D$, let the $B$ complete
evaluation blocks be independent, each with $n_0$ rows. Let $\Theta$ be a frozen
family of size $m$, and suppose each block has a sufficient conditional
dependency graph with a proper coloring using at most $\chi$ colors.
The score fits and policy--auditor functions are fixed given $\mathcal D$.
For each $\theta$, write $m_{bi}(\theta)=\mathbb E[Z_{bi}(\theta)\mid\mathcal D]$,
and assume
\[
 \frac{1}{n_0}\sum_i m_{bi}(\theta)=M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)
 \quad\text{for every block }b,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E[|Z_{bi}(\theta)-m_{bi}(\theta)|^p\mid\mathcal D]
 \le \nu_\theta^p,
 \quad 1<p\le2,
\]
where the finite envelopes $\nu_\theta$ are known and
$\mathcal D$-measurable. These are the stable-target assumptions of the
independent-block certificate, not assumptions inferred from an observed
autocorrelation or from row-wise cross-fitting.

\begin{theorem}[Exact binomial calibration of graph/block MOM]
\label{thm:c0-exact-majority}
Fix $0<\eta<1/2$ and $0<\delta<1$ before evaluation. For an odd integer $K$,
define
\[
 t(K,\eta)
 =\sum_{j=(K+1)/2}^{K}{K\choose j}\eta^j(1-\eta)^{K-j}.
\]
Choose the smallest odd $K$ satisfying $B\ge2K$ and $m t(K,\eta)\le\delta$,
if one exists. Partition all $B$ blocks deterministically into $K$ disjoint
groups with sizes $L_g\in\{\lfloor B/K\rfloor,\lceil B/K\rceil\}$, and let
$L_{\min}=\lfloor B/K\rfloor$. Let $T_g(\theta)$ be the mean of the actual
$L_g$ block means, and let $\widehat M_{\rm MOM}(\theta)$ be the middle order
statistic of the $K$ values. Set
\[
 a_\theta
 =\left(\frac4\eta\right)^{1/p}\nu_\theta
   \left(\frac{\chi}{L_{\min}n_0}\right)^{(p-1)/p}.
\]
Then
\[
 \mathbb P\!\left(
  |\widehat M_{\rm MOM}(\theta)-M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)|\le a_\theta
  \text{ for all }\theta\in\Theta\,\middle|\,\mathcal D\right)
 \ge 1-\delta.
\]
If no such $K$ exists, this construction issues no certificate. No retained
block or row is discarded to force feasibility.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a fixed $\theta$, center each row at $m_{bi}(\theta)$. Conditional
von Bahr--Esseen with constant at most $2$ inside each independent color class,
followed by convexity across the at most $\chi$ color sums, gives
\[
 \mathbb E[|\bar Z_b(\theta)-M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)|^p\mid\mathcal D]
 \le2\nu_\theta^p(\chi/n_0)^{p-1}.
\]
A second application across the independent complete blocks in group $g$ gives
\[
 \mathbb E[|T_g(\theta)-M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)|^p\mid\mathcal D]
 \le4\nu_\theta^p
       \left(\frac{\chi}{L_gn_0}\right)^{p-1}
 \le4\nu_\theta^p
       \left(\frac{\chi}{L_{\min}n_0}\right)^{p-1}.
\]
If $\nu_\theta=0$, the claim is immediate. Otherwise Markov's inequality
bounds each group's failure probability at threshold $a_\theta$ by $\eta$.
Their bad-event indicators are independent conditional on $\mathcal D$,
because the groups use disjoint independent blocks. Their probabilities
$p_g\le\eta$ need not be identical. For independent auxiliary uniforms $U_g$,
the indicators $\mathbf1\{U_g\le p_g\}$ have the same conditional joint law,
and are bounded pointwise by independent $\mathbf1\{U_g\le\eta\}$.
Their sum is therefore stochastically dominated by $\operatorname{Bin}(K,\eta)$.

When fewer than $(K+1)/2$ groups fail, a strict majority of group means lies
inside $[M_{\mathcal D}-a_\theta,M_{\mathcal D}+a_\theta]$, which contains
the median. The single-candidate failure probability is thus at most
$t(K,\eta)$. A union bound over the frozen family gives $m t(K,\eta)\le\delta$;
independence across candidates is not needed. Stable block targets ensure
that all group means have the same target even when their lengths differ.
The conditional statement integrates to an unconditional statement for
$M_{\mathcal D}$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Numerical rule and scope.}
The first comparison fixes $\eta=1/8$, retaining the constant $32^{1/p}$.
For $m=16$ and $\delta=.05$, exact rational summation gives
\[
 16t(9,1/8)=0.039716482162475586<.05,
 \qquad 16t(7,1/8)=0.099822998046875>.05.
\]
Thus $K=9$ replaces the sufficient conservative choice
$\lceil8\log(2m/\delta)\rceil=52$. At $B=128$, $L_{\min}$ changes from $2$
to $14$, reducing the radius by $7^{-(p-1)/p}$ with every other input fixed.
This improvement alone does not guarantee a useful interval when the known
moment envelope is large. Exact arithmetic is used for the tail-budget
comparison; a rounded floating-point tail is only a diagnostic.

For even $K$ with the average-of-two-middle-values convention, fewer than
$K/2$ bad groups suffice, so the valid sufficient tail threshold is $K/2$.
The new selector restricts to odd $K$. A design-only selection is fixed in the
conditional proof; selection using evaluation outcomes or oracle truth requires
additional justification or simultaneous adjustment. Unequal block targets,
future-outcome-conditioned designs, mutually trained nuisance fits, and
uncontrolled cross-block dependence remain outside this theorem.

\subsection{A separate bounded-outcome graph variance certificate}
\label{sec:c0-bounded-variance}

For this alternative, let $\mathcal D$ be legitimate exogenous pre-action
information as above. Conditional on $\mathcal D$, the evaluation blocks are
independent, but their sizes and their conditional target means may differ.
Within block $b$, a sufficient dependency graph has a verified proper coloring
using $\chi_b$ colors. The frozen family $\Theta$ has $m$ members.
For each unit $u$, require $Y_u(s)\in[0,1]$, consistency, conditional
exchangeability, and exact exposure probabilities $e_u(s)$ on the same finite
state space as the nonnegative target distribution $q_u(s)$.
Require $q_u(s)>0\Rightarrow e_u(s)>0$.
The functions $h_u,f_u,\widehat\mu_u(s)$ are $\mathcal D$-measurable, with
$|h_u|\le1$ and $\widehat\mu_u(s)\in[0,1]$.

Define
\begin{align*}
 Z_u(\theta)
 &=h_u\left[\sum_s q_u(s)\widehat\mu_u(s)-f_u
       +\frac{q_u(S_u)}{e_u(S_u)}
        \{Y_u-\widehat\mu_u(S_u)\}\right],\\
 M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)
 &=\frac1N\sum_u h_u
       \left[\sum_s q_u(s)\mathbb E[Y_u(s)\mid\mathcal D]-f_u\right],\\
 v_u(\theta)
 &=h_u^2\sum_{s:e_u(s)>0}\frac{q_u(s)^2}{e_u(s)},
 \qquad
 V_\theta=\frac1{N^2}\sum_b\chi_b\sum_{u\in b}v_u(\theta).
\end{align*}
Terms with $q_u(s)=e_u(s)=0$ contribute zero. Unsupported target mass is an
identification failure, not a reason to clip the propensity numerically.

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-outcome graph Chebyshev certificate]
\label{thm:c0-bounded-chebyshev}
Under the preceding assumptions, let
$\widehat M_{\rm mean}(\theta)=N^{-1}\sum_u Z_u(\theta)$ and fix
$\delta\in(0,1)$ before evaluation. The intervals
\[
 \left[\widehat M_{\rm mean}(\theta)-r_\theta,
       \widehat M_{\rm mean}(\theta)+r_\theta\right],
 \qquad
 r_\theta=\sqrt{\frac{mV_\theta}{\delta}},
\]
simultaneously cover $M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)$ for all $\theta\in\Theta$
with conditional probability at least $1-\delta$ given $\mathcal D$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exact-propensity DR identity with a fixed permissible fit gives
$\mathbb E[\widehat M_{\rm mean}(\theta)\mid\mathcal D]
=M_{\mathcal D}(\theta)$. Write the score as a $\mathcal D$-measurable
direct term plus $h_uR_u$, where
$R_u=q_u(S_u)\{Y_u-\widehat\mu_u(S_u)\}/e_u(S_u)$.
The residual is bounded in absolute value by one before multiplication by
the weight. Consequently,
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Z_u\mid\mathcal D)
 =h_u^2\operatorname{Var}(R_u\mid\mathcal D)
 \le h_u^2\mathbb E[R_u^2\mid\mathcal D]
 \le h_u^2\sum_{s:e_u(s)>0}\frac{q_u(s)^2}{e_u(s)}=v_u.
\]
Center every row score and sum within each color class in block $b$.
Variance additivity within the independent color classes and
Cauchy--Schwarz across their possibly dependent sums imply
\[
 \operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{u\in b}Z_u\,\middle|\,\mathcal D\right)
 \le\chi_b\sum_{u\in b}\operatorname{Var}(Z_u\mid\mathcal D)
 \le\chi_b\sum_{u\in b}v_u.
\]
Independence of the entire block vectors then yields
$\operatorname{Var}(\widehat M_{\rm mean}\mid\mathcal D)\le V_\theta$.
For $V_\theta>0$, conditional Chebyshev bounds a candidate's failure by
$V_\theta/r_\theta^2=\delta/m$. If $V_\theta=0$, its centered estimator
vanishes almost surely. Union bounding over the family proves the claim.
No equal-block-target or MOM drift argument is used: the raw-row sample mean
has the raw-row fixed-design target even when block means differ.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Known support and separate interpretation.}
For a binary fixed-design auditor, the target surely belongs to
\[
 \left[-\frac1N\sum_u h_uf_u,
        \frac1N\sum_u h_u(1-f_u)\right].
\]
Intersecting a valid confidence interval with this known set preserves its
coverage. Raw and support-intersected endpoints should both be retained;
returning the whole support range provides no information beyond the model.
An empty intersection remains an empty confidence set and counts as a
coverage failure. A subgroup ratio additionally requires positive known mass.

This is a bounded-reward, exact-e sample-mean theorem, not a replacement for
finite-$p$ MOM when finite variance is unavailable, and not a proof of fully
adaptive pathwise inference. The initial implementation still uses the original
registered stable-target simulator; the theorem's allowance for heterogeneous
means is not a claim that arbitrary external logs satisfy its assumptions.
A small supported propensity can make $V_\theta$ very large. Missing graph
edges, a permanent cross-block shock, evaluation-trained fits, or a future
outcome-dependent conditioning field invalidate the relevant assumptions even
if a particular observed log passes schema checks. Graph coloring plus
Chebyshev is a standard argument, not in itself a major novelty claim.

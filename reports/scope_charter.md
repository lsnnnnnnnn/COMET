## Three distinct claim levels

1. **Historical established baseline / E0:** independent evaluation blocks,
   common block targets for MOM, exact logging, sufficient within-block graphs,
   known moment envelopes, fixed or independent nuisance. Preserve all 6,000
   logs and 1,134,000 records and the original 17-test family. Additional E0
   outcome draws in this workstream must remain **zero**. The nine-page C-STAT
   proposal remains a historical report.
2. **Required new core:** heterogeneous fixed-design means; a global sufficient
   conditional dependency graph of the whole residual vector; exact supported
   exposure laws; valid conditional residual moments; a finite frozen query
   family; residual clipping with explicit bias and concentration; local
   support/moments/coloring, signed paired contrasts, evidence costs and
   safe-and-useful decisions. Each certified path needs its own completed proof.
3. **Optional:** fixed-horizon predictable sequential inference, subject to a
   separate complete theorem. Failure of this extension cannot delay the core.

Independent training/discovery information may be included in the conditioning
field only when its random sources are independent of certification. A schema
can check a supplied coloring or incidence representation; it cannot establish
that an unobserved shared shock or training leakage does not exist. Graph
sufficiency follows from the declared probability model. Unequal means, one
connected graph and overlapping sources are allowed by the new theorem.

No unrestricted adaptive-history, mutual-cross-fit, unknown graph/scale,
arbitrary-stopping, infinite-class, long-run safety, or graph-by-graph minimax
sharpness claim is authorized by the core theorem. Exact exposure marginals for
an interference policy require a compatible full assignment law. Shared outcome
noise alone is not interference. There is no imposed numerical propensity floor;
a finite design can nevertheless have a positive minimum and poor precision.

## 3. Scope that must remain fixed as the research objective

**Title:** COMET: Counterfactual Moment Auditing under Weak Overlap and Graph
Dependence.

**Question:** which heterogeneous subgroup/policy moments can be certified from
selective dependent feedback, what determines their evidence cost, and which
useful decisions follow from those certificates?

**Core probability model:** a legitimate exogenous conditioning field D plus
independent training/discovery information; exact supported exposure laws;
known valid conditional residual moment envelopes; a finite frozen family;
and a sufficient conditional dependency graph of the *whole* score/residual
vector. Unequal means and one connected graph are allowed. Independent repeated
macro-blocks, common block means, and exact copied clique records are not required.

**Main estimator:** separate the known direct term from the random weighted
residual; clip the residual with a D-measurable threshold; pay explicit
worst-case bias plus graph concentration. Keep a sharp bounded-outcome
untruncated branch. The old MOM is a reference, not the default new core.

**Substantive questions:** query-local support/moments/graph; direct paired
contrasts; subgroup/overlap/dependence lower-bound subclass and power boundary;
independent discovery; safe-and-useful finite-policy return.

**Outside core claims:** unrestricted adaptive histories, mutual cross-fitting,
unknown graph/scale recovered distribution-free from one log, unlimited-class
fairness, exact graph-by-graph minimax confidence-width optimality, optional
stopping, and future-distribution/long-term performative safety. An optional
predictable fixed-horizon theorem must not delay the core deliverable.

## 4. Mandatory scientific corrections before new large runs

### 4.1 Strong bounded baseline, demonstrated analytically in A

For the frozen A design, q=e=(.5,.5), muhat=.5 and f=.25 make Z=Y-.25.
There are 8,192 independent bounded observations. Two-sided Hoeffding gives
r_H=sqrt(log(40)/(2*8192))=0.015005043614373761 at alpha=.05, m=1.
The target is .2375 and threshold .2, so gamma=.0375>2r_H=.030010087228747522.
On the coverage event the lower bound exceeds .2. Thus under this DGP the
Hoeffding certificate has a >=95% theoretical detection guarantee. This is
not a newly measured power result and it is not a claim for m=16 or chi>1.

Add a deterministic test of this fixture and its general range formula. Use
this as a motivation for stronger comparisons, not as a retrofit to the old
17-test family. The old A empirical detection of 1.2% is not an information
limit of the problem.

For midpoint nuisance and bounded Y in [0,1], use the legal residual bound
.5 for *all* eligible methods, not 1 for baselines and .5 only for COMET.
For general bounded fits use max(|L-muhat|,|U-muhat|). Give baselines the same
support, graph, exact e, moment information, nuisance and total error budget.

### 4.2 No benefit manufactured from larger independent sample counts

Build source-overlap graphs first and prove their sufficiency. A cycle of
shared primitive shocks is not a collection of replicated clusters and must
not be silently converted into one giant independent observation or many
fake independent blocks. Removing an inactive row does not erase a primitive
that correlates two retained rows. Source-to-score incidence is part of the
model contract; pairwise covariance alone is inadequate.

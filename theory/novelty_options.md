# Novelty decision for Gate A

**Choose option C as the primary research direction:** finite-family counterfactual auditing and one-step repair under an explicit local-exposure model. The Gate A+B result is a proof-matched baseline for this direction, not a completed claim of novel adaptive inference. Graph-aware finite-p MOM aggregation, a union bound, exact-e DR identification, clique replication, and pessimistic selection are not individually new contributions.

Original source references below are to commit `58ad7bd`: the main certificate is `paper/sections/04_theory.tex:19–37` and `paper/sections/full/04_theory.tex:25–53`; repair is `paper/sections/05_discover_repair.tex:29–37`; the adaptive target is `paper/sections/02_setup.tex:26–33`; graph reduction is `paper/sections/04_theory.tex:6–15`. Reading these as a single established adaptive theorem exceeds the current proof.

| Direction | What would distinguish it | Gate A status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: adaptive pathwise graph-aware finite-p inference | A predictable robust statistic for evolving targets, with a proved filtration, simultaneous/local-interference validity, and explicit moment and confidence budgets | Unproved. Independent-block MOM does not provide it; adaptive and time-varying off-policy inference already exist | Retain as a separate research theorem, not the first certified API |
| B: computable bounded-reward CI with heterogeneous graph effects | A materially tighter computable graph/moment functional, with a comparison identifying regimes where it improves existing graph concentration | Computable conservative bounded-reward envelope is proved in the clean model; tighter heterogeneous-graph result is not proved | Useful secondary direction; do not call replacing chi by fractional coloring novel by itself |
| C: finite-family local-exposure auditing plus repair | Precisely identified joint exposure, stochastic dependence justified by the assignment/outcome model, auditable certificates, and useful intervention/constraint trade-offs | Clean independent-block certificate and deterministic repair implication are justified; broad local-interference performance and repair experiments await Gates C+D | Primary direction, with novelty still to be established by a precise new theorem or compelling evidence |

The concrete current result is simultaneous fixed-window coverage conditional on an exogenous design, with known sufficient graphs, fresh independent macro-blocks, fixed nuisances or an independent pilot, exact exposure probabilities, and known score moments. This is narrower because the missing independence and conditioning steps are mathematical obligations, not implementation inconveniences. It does not establish closed-loop deployment safety. The repair proposition is valid algebra on an already valid simultaneous event; its existence does not prove that event.

## Verified prior-work comparisons

Checked 2026-11-12 against primary pages and, where noted, full-text sections. The comparisons are scoped to those results and are not an exhaustive priority search.

| Primary source | Verified relevant result and assumptions | Consequence for COMET |
|---|---|---|
| [Waudby-Smith, Wu, Ramdas, Karampatziakis & Mineiro, arXiv v3 (2024 revision)](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.10768v3), Algorithm 1, Section 1.1, Theorem 2 and Proposition 3 | Time-uniform inference for time-varying off-policy means, with bounded rewards for these theorems, exact logging ratios, predictable reward fits, and no uniform bound on importance weights. The contextual-bandit interpretation assumes exogenously varying context/reward distributions; their Section 1.1 distinguishes conditional-on-past values from full trajectory counterfactuals when this fails | Adaptive logging, unbounded weights, and changing means are not sufficient novelty claims. Their temporal validity is stronger than the present offline MOM result. COMET must justify a distinct interference/dependency-graph setting, not attribute full trajectory safety to either construction |
| [Mineiro (2022)](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.11133v1), Definition 1, Theorem 2 and Corollary 1 | A lower confidence sequence for the running average conditional mean of adapted nonnegative observations with a bounded conditional mean. Its construction and mixtures accommodate heavy right tails and moment adaptation under this more restrictive mean/sign structure | “Heavy tails plus changing means” already has a sequential treatment. It does not automatically certify arbitrary signed COMET scores or a data-driven MOM envelope; any reduction must verify sign/mean assumptions |
| [Høgsgaard & Paudice, ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/hogsgaard25a.html), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14673) | Uniform MOM estimation of function-class means under finite moments of order $p\in(1,2]$, with a new symmetrization/discretization analysis of sample complexity | Simultaneity and finite-p MOM are established ingredients. A frozen finite-family union bound alone is a simpler specialization, not evidence of a stronger uniform-learning contribution |
| [Janson (2004), publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/rsa.20008), [author preprint, Section 2 and Theorem 2.1](https://api.newton.ac.uk/website/v0/events/preprints/NI02024) | Concentration by decomposing dependent sums into independent subsets; integral and fractional covers are explicit. The bounded-summand exponential bounds are not themselves finite-p MOM theorems | Coloring and fractional coloring are established dependence reductions. COMET must establish that its graph is sufficient after the exact conditioning used; identifiers alone cannot supply that proof |
| [Lerasle & Oliveira (2011), Sections 2 and 6, Proposition 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1112.3914) | MOM/robust empirical means extend to stationary mixing data through block coupling. Their beta-mixing bound uses summability and fourth moments; a phi-mixing variant uses finite second moments. Coupling failure is an additional probability term | Mixing plus MOM is prior art. This does not supply COMET's finite-p adaptive-path theorem or permit an unknown mixing coefficient to become a numeric radius |

Waudby-Smith et al. and Mineiro handle evolving conditional means with time-uniform guarantees under their stated models; the current independent-block COMET theorem is weaker on that axis. A graph/interference extension could still be valuable, but must be proved and compared on a common estimand. The above conclusion is an inference from the stated theorem scopes, not a claim that those papers prove COMET's causal model.

## Claims available now and claims still needed

**Proved in the Gate A contract:** exact-e DR identity for a fixed permissible nuisance; graph moment reduction under strong conditional independence; explicit conservative MOM radius for independent evaluation macro-blocks with stable conditional block targets; known bounded-reward score envelope; the corrected expected-risk clique lower bound. The finite-library feasibility/utility implication is proved conditional on simultaneous coverage. These establish mathematical soundness, not theoretical originality.

**Not proved here:** arbitrary adaptive logged-history coverage; mutual cross-fit conditional independence; a uniform conditional scale derived from an unconditional moment; tail-scale adaptation from evaluation data; graph-specific sharpness for every sparse graph; deploy-and-feedback safety; unknown-drift or unknown-coupling certification. Gates A+B do not produce empirical evidence for the final paper's coverage or utility claims.

For option C to become the paper's technical contribution, the next research step must specify a nontrivial joint assignment/exposure model and a sufficient graph, state exactly which counterfactual moments are identified, and either prove a substantive result beyond the standard composition or establish a carefully delimited operational contribution through later experiments. Do not start those experiments in the first run. An exact small-slate probability check is a prerequisite for identification, not by itself a novelty result.
